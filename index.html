# WINNING TEAM PROJECT · SMART URBAN LIVING HACKATHON · OCT 6–7, 2026

Lakad

Built with Team Lakad Kinetic Development at iACADEMY MPH. Lakad makes walking around Makati safer and helps the city turn reports into trackable action.

## Highlights

  • WINNER — Poland–Philippines IT Summit
  • Team Lakad Kinetic Development contributor
  • Compares Fastest, Walkable, and Accessible routes that avoid hazards and accessibility barriers
  • Geotagged reports, face blurring, and an SOS video flow
  • City dashboard groups reports into trackable tickets with insights and traffic views

## Built with

Flutter (Android) · React · TypeScript · FastAPI · Python · SQLite · Mapbox · TomTom · OpenRouteService
